## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the best way to get materials for Slop Tower Defense crafting?
Play the modes and claim the rewards shown in your current game version, then check the crafting screen before spending materials. The exact recipe and reward loop can change with updates.

### Do I need to be a certain level to craft items?
The reviewed sources do not establish a universal level gate for every recipe. Check the current crafting screen for any requirement attached to the item you want to make.

### What should I do if my units aren't hitting the enemies?
Double-check if your units are damage dealers or money farmers. In Slop Tower Defense, some units are designed solely to generate cash and will not attack enemies. Ensure you have a mix of dedicated attackers placed along the path.

### Where can I find new updates or news on crafting recipes?
The safest place to check for new content and potential crafting changes is the official Roblox game page and its linked community channels. Re-check the recipe in-game after an update instead of relying on an old guide.
