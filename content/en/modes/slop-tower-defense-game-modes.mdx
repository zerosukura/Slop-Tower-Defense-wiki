## Strategic Tips for Every Mode

To succeed in any *Slop Tower Defense* game mode, you must balance your economy with your defense. Here are some actionable tips derived from experienced player walkthroughs:

### 1. Optimize Your Starting Economy
Do not rush to place every unit you have at the start. Many players find success by placing a dedicated money-farming unit early on. Remember that some units are specifically designed for economy rather than damage; ensure you have a mix of damage-dealing units to cover the lanes.

### 2. Strategic Unit Placement
If your base is taking damage, it is often a sign that your units are not placed within range of the enemy path. Use the "skip wave" feature cautiously. While skipping waves allows you to earn cash faster, it also increases the pressure on your defenses. Only skip when you are confident that your current setup can handle the incoming threat.

### 3. Use Your Resources Wisely
Save your hard-earned cash for units that fit the current mode and your existing roster. Upgrading an available unit can be more useful than placing several low-level units, but compare the actual costs and stats shown in-game.
