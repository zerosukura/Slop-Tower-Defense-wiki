## Understanding Slop Tower Defense Units

In *Slop Tower Defense*, building a formidable team is the key to surviving increasingly difficult waves and successfully completing raids. Units serve as your primary defense, and their effectiveness is determined by their rarity, type, and whether they have been upgraded or evolved. 

Players often focus on summoning, crafting, and trading to obtain top-tier units. Based on player experiences, understanding the mechanics behind these systems is essential for progression. Whether you are aiming for a Celestial Stalker or the highly sought-after Moon Blossom, mastering the unit acquisition process requires a mix of resource management and strategic planning.

## The Summoning System and Rarities

Summoning is the primary method for acquiring new units in *Slop Tower Defense*. The game features various crates and summon pools that offer different chances at obtaining Mythic, Godly, and Secret rarity units.

According to player reports, summoning is a game of probability. While it is possible to pull high-rarity units like Godlies or Secrets, the drop rates are intentionally low. Players often utilize "pity" systems, where the game guarantees a higher-rarity pull after a certain number of failed attempts. 

Key takeaways from the summoning experience include:
* **Confetti Effects:** Players often look for specific visual cues, such as confetti, which indicate that a high-rarity unit (like a Godly or Secret) has been summoned.
* **Shiny and Void Variants:** Beyond standard units, players aim for "Shiny" or "Void" versions. These variants are highly coveted and often require significant luck or multiple summons to obtain.
* **Resource Management:** Gems and stars are the primary currencies used for summoning. It is often recommended to save these resources rather than spending them immediately, especially when aiming for limited-time event units.

## Crafting and Evolving Your Units

If you are unable to summon the specific units you need, the game’s crafting system offers an alternative path. The crafting UI provides a clear indicator when you have enough materials to create a specific unit, which is a helpful feature for tracking your progress.

Crafting often requires specific ingredients, which may be obtained through:
* **Weekly Exclusives:** Some units, like the Candy Blossom, are exclusive to weekly rotations.
* **Trade:** When crafting materials are rare or difficult to obtain, players may turn to the trading market. Engaging in trades can be expensive, often requiring the use of "slot bucks" or other rare assets.
* **Evolution:** Certain units can be evolved into more powerful forms. Evolution typically requires a set amount of base units and specific upgrade items, such as scythes or other specialized materials.

## Strategic Unit Placement and Raid Performance

Once you have acquired a balanced team, the next challenge is deploying them effectively during gameplay. Raids are particularly demanding, and a team lacking in crowd control or high-damage output will likely struggle against late-wave enemies.

### Tips for Raid Success
* **Prioritize Stun and Slow:** As noted by experienced players, units that provide stun and slow effects are invaluable. They allow your high-damage units more time to eliminate threats.
* **Tanky Units:** Having units that can withstand damage is essential for maintaining your defense line.
* **Synergy:** Don't just place your highest rarity units. Consider how their abilities interact. Combining a unit with splash damage with one that provides a slowing effect can significantly improve your wave clear efficiency.

If you find yourself losing in raids, consider checking your unit upgrades. Even a high-tier unit will perform poorly if it is not fully upgraded with the necessary items collected during your gameplay sessions.

## Managing Your Inventory

As your collection grows, managing your inventory becomes a necessity. Players frequently accumulate duplicate units from crates and summons. 

* **Selling Duplicates:** Selling duplicate units is a standard way to regain resources. It is generally safe to sell duplicate legendaries if you have already locked the versions you intend to keep.
* **Locking Units:** Always lock your essential units (especially your Shiny or Void variants) to prevent accidentally selling them during a cleanup session.
* **Tracking Progress:** Keep an eye on your "after-win" rewards and other mission-based criteria, as these often provide the materials needed for your next evolution.
